Privacy Policy

Effective October 13, 2026

{{APP_NAME}} is a free math practice website. It runs entirely in your web browser. It has no server, no user database and no advertising. This policy explains what data the app uses and where it goes.

Data the app accesses

  • Your Google account name and email address. These are shown on screen so you know which account is signed in.
  • Files in your Google Drive that {{APP_NAME}} created. The app asks only for the drive.file permission. That lets it see and change only the files it creates itself; it cannot see any of your other Drive files.

Data the app stores

Everything is saved in a folder named {{APP_NAME}} in your own Google Drive:

  • the tests you add in admin mode
  • the test in progress
  • finished tests, with the questions asked, the answers chosen, timing, scores and difficulty levels
  • which questions have already been used

Your browser also keeps the temporary Google sign-in token, which is cleared when the tab closes, and your email address, which pre-fills the next sign-in. Signing out clears both.

Sharing

Your Google account information and test data are never sent to the developer of {{APP_NAME}} or to anyone else. They move only between your browser and Google Drive. Google handles it under the Google Privacy Policy. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts. Like any website host, these services can see standard request details such as your IP address.

Analytics

{{APP_NAME}} uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages and features are used. Google Analytics uses cookies and collects standard usage data such as pages viewed, approximate location, device and browser type. It never receives your name, email address, Google Drive files, answers or scores. Google processes this data under the How Google uses information from sites that use its services policy. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Google API Services User Data Policy

{{APP_NAME}}'s use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the app's features in your browser. It is never sold, never used for advertising, never sent to analytics, never transferred to others, and never read by people.

Children

{{APP_NAME}} is designed for students. A parent, guardian or teacher should set it up and sign it in to a Google account they manage or approve. The app collects no information beyond what is described above.

Your control

  • Delete the {{APP_NAME}} folder in Google Drive to erase all stored data.
  • Remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy will be posted on this page. Questions can be asked through the project's GitHub issues.
